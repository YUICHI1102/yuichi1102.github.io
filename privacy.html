Genji Uploader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Information we access

When a TikTok account authorizes this app through TikTok Login Kit, we access the account's basic profile information and receive OAuth access and refresh tokens for that account.

2. How information is used

This information is used only to identify the connected account and to upload video drafts to that account's TikTok inbox, at the operator's direction, through the Content Posting API.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not access data of people who have not authorized the app.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored securely and are used only to operate the tool.

5. Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings. To request deletion of stored tokens, contact us at the address below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page.
